Research

I work in computational biology, at the intersection of single-cell & spatial multi-omics and AI agents for science. Biological data now arrive from many studies, modalities, and technologies, but rarely measured all together. My research asks how to make these fragmented measurements add up. I build methods that samples across studies and modalities, the features a technology did not measure, and analysis from a natural-language question to a validated result.

Harmonize across studies
Embed single-cell samples from different studies and modalities (scRNA-seq, scATAC-seq, proteomics) in one shared space, without paired measurements.
Predict what wasn't measured
Impute unmeasured genes and proteins in spatial data using foundation-model gene embeddings and transformer-based spatial attention.
Automate analysis
Multi-agent systems that turn natural-language requests into validated epigenomic analyses, scripts, and reports.
