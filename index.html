Research

I work in computational biology, at the intersection of single-cell & spatial multi-omics and AI agents for science. Biological data now arrive from many studies, modalities, and technologies, but rarely measured all together. My research asks how to make these fragmented measurements add up. I build methods that samples across studies and modalities, the features a technology did not measure, and analysis from a natural-language question to a validated result.

Harmonize across studies
Embed single-cell samples from different studies and assays (RNA, chromatin accessibility, protein) in one shared space, without paired measurements.

Predict what wasn't measured

Research

  1. SampleDisco: Harmonizing Single-Cell Data Across Studies and Modalities to Reveal Sample Heterogeneity

    Harry Jiang, Z. Ji, Y. Wang, M. Jiang, Y. Man, Hongkai Ji

    Single-cell multi-omics · Sample embedding · Data integration · Trajectory inference

    Places single-cell samples from different studies and assays (RNA, chromatin accessibility, protein) on one shared map by describing which cell states each sample contains and how its cells shift within each cell type; first overall against 13 other methods in every benchmark panel.

    Read moreTutorialPyPI

  2. RESONATE: Predicting unmeasured genes and proteins in spatial omics from foundation models and single-cell references

    Harry Jiang, Hongkai Ji, Uthsav Chitra

    Spatial transcriptomics · Gene imputation · Foundation models · Single-cell reference · Cross-omics prediction

    Predicts genes and proteins missing from spatial omics panels by describing each gene with a foundation-model embedding, so one model works for any gene and keeps 86% of its accuracy without a reference. Most accurate on all ten datasets tested, versus 14 published methods.

    Read moreTutorialPoster

  3. ChromaPilot: A Domain-Guided Multi-Agent Framework for Reproducible Multimodal Epigenomic Analysis

    Hua Jiang*, M. Jiang*, Q. You*, Y. Hu, L. Qi, W. Zhou, Hongkai Ji

    Epigenomics · LLM agents · Multiplexed chromatin profiling · Workflow automation · Reproducibility

    An AI agent system for multiplexed chromatin data that encodes expert knowledge as validated tools and a dependency graph, letting the language model only orchestrate them. It matched expert analyses (1.00 on a 0–1 scale) where Claude Code and ChatGPT alone scored 0.15 and 0.01.

  1. Improving the thermostability of glycosyltransferase UGT94E13 by rational design for highly efficient biosynthesis of rebaudioside M8

    Hua Jiang*, Guangyao Li*, Weilun Zhang, Zhiwei Deng, Mengliang Yang, Changmei Liu, Zhengshan Luo, Yan Zhang, Yijian Rao, Zhenbo Yuan

    Food Bioscience 2025Protein engineering · Enzyme thermostability · Glycosyltransferase · Molecular dynamics · Biocatalysis

    Computational design plus structural and conservation filtering turned a fragile stevia-sweetener glycosyltransferase into a variant with a 5.4 °C higher melting temperature, a 14.8× longer half-life, while retaining 108% of the parent enzyme's activity.
